# Work item 101 (Bug): Discount codes also reduce the shipping charge

**Paste this title when /start asks:** Discount codes also reduce the shipping charge

## Description

When a customer uses a discount code, the discount is taken off the shipping charge as well as the items. Discounts should apply to the items only. Shipping is always charged in full.

## Repro steps

1. Run orders-api.
2. GET `/api/orders/1002/summary` (order 1002 uses code SAVE10, two hoodies at 40.00 USD, standard shipping 7.99 USD).
3. Look at `discount` and `total`.

## Expected

- Subtotal 80.00 USD, shipping 7.99 USD
- Discount 8.00 USD (10% of 80.00)
- Total 79.99 USD

## Actual

- Discount 8.80 USD (10% of 87.99)
- Total 79.19 USD

## Acceptance criteria

- Discount is calculated on the subtotal only, never on shipping.
- Orders without a discount code are unchanged.
