# Work item L1: Bulk discount for large orders

- Type: Feature
- State: New
- Link: none (local work item)
- Fetched: Pasted by engineer

## Description

Customers who order a lot should pay less. Orders with 10 or more items in total (adding up the quantity on every line) get 5% off the subtotal. Smaller orders are priced as today. Sales expects to change the threshold and the rate later.

## Acceptance criteria

- An order with 10 or more items in total gets 5% off its subtotal.
- An order with fewer than 10 items is priced as today.
- The threshold and the rate can be changed in one place without touching the pricing rule.
